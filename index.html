Digital Customs Administration

ASYCUDA World & Customs Digital Services

We support businesses in preparing, filing, and managing customs documentation and digital customs processes to eliminate demurrage, reduce delays, and prevent compliance violations across Iraqi entry points.

1

Single Administrative Document (SAD) Preparation

Direct electronic entry into the ASYCUDA World system for import, export, and transit declarations across federal and KRI border stations.

2

HS Code Classification & Valuation Pre-Audit

Expert tariff code assignment under the WCO Harmonized System to prevent re-assessment disputes, penalties, or misdeclaration fines.

Duty & Tax Calculation Support

Facilitation of physical cargo audits, X-ray scanning, and quarantine sampling with customs authorities to achieve fast green/yellow channel release.
